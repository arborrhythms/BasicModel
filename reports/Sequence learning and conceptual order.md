# One Recursion Hosts Two Distinct Operations

The literature does not support a single equivalent mechanism for sequence learning and conceptual order; it supports one recursive loop, scored by one predictive or compression objective, that alternates two operations which formal results keep distinct. Composition (chunking units that follow one another) builds parts within wholes and is conservative; class formation (grouping units that occur in the same contexts) builds kinds over instances, is a generalising hypothesis, and cannot be defined from composition without adding variables. Brains show the same split: learned lists are stored as item-to-position bindings and not as successor links, children recite the count list long before it carries cardinal meaning, and sequence knowledge and conceptual knowledge dissociate, although the hippocampal-entorhinal system does turn sequence statistics into cluster and distance codes. Symbols are not what lets thought replace behavior, since rats and model-based agents run covert trial and error without them; the defensible claim is narrower, that a symbol-like token is a candidate for what lets thought operate on thought. Design rule 1 is well supported as chunking, but it yields only part-whole structure and cannot generalise alone. Design rule 2 is formally exact (it is the quotient of a preorder by its symmetric part, with a counterpart in non-antisymmetric mereology), yet mutual serial prediction is the statistic of a composite or a loop and not of a category; adult humans show the reverse link after one-way exposure, two language-trained chimpanzees showed no symmetry after long two-way training, and no experiment tests the rule directly. Three statuses are kept apart throughout: established, contested, and unverified, the last meaning that the research notes confirmed a citation but gave its content from memory or from a secondary description.

## Formal theory separates chunking from class formation

The guess under review bundles three claims: that sequence learning and conceptual order use the same operation, that the operation is recursive, and that symbols are what turn it into thought. Formal language theory, order theory and grammar induction address the first two in a setting where "one operation or two" can be put to proof, and their answer is consistent. The recursion is shared. The operation is not.

**Chunking is conservative.** Sequitur, Re-Pair and byte-pair encoding each apply one recursive move, replacing a repeated adjacent pair with a new symbol, and the grammar that results generates exactly the input string and nothing else ([Nevill-Manning & Witten 1997](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/9709102); [Re-Pair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Re-Pair); [Byte pair encoding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byte_pair_encoding)). **Class formation is ampliative.** In Bayesian model merging, merging two nonterminals is the step that makes a grammar generate more than its sample, and chunking was added only because merging alone gives no hierarchy; the worked derivation of a^n b^n takes two chunks and then two merges, and in the experiments lexical categories came from merging, phrasal categories from chunking, and recursion from the two together ([Stolcke & Omohundro 1994](https://arxiv.org/abs/cmp-lg/9409010)). Gold's theorem shows that such leaps cannot be made safely from positive examples across any class containing all finite languages and one infinite one ([Language identification in the limit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language_identification_in_the_limit)). The positive result that rescues learnability does so by restriction: substitutable context-free languages are identifiable in polynomial time from positive data, and the class excludes even a^n b^n ([Clark & Eyraud 2007](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume8/clark07a/clark07a.pdf)). Algebraically the two operations are the conjunction-like and disjunction-like constructors, and in Post's lattice AND and OR generate distinct clones, neither definable from the other without negation ([Post's lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post%27s_lattice)). The research notes add a dynamical point that follows from these facts: a quotient is idempotent and cannot generate level after level, while a chunk-only learner never generalises, so alternation is the minimal recipe that is both productive and generalising. That last step is the researchers' own argument. They found no published theorem that the two operations are irreducible in general.

Every induction system that generalises confirms the pattern. ADIOS alternates a step that distils statistically significant sub-paths into new units with a step that collects the units filling one slot between a shared prefix and suffix into an equivalence class ([Solan et al. 2005](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1187953/)). The hierarchical variable model keeps a chunking test (a consecutive pair significantly correlated at p = 0.05) and adds a second operation that proposes a variable for distinct chunks sharing the same preceding and following chunks. Its sequence likelihood tracks human recall time (**R = 0.86, n = 112**), and **the chunk-only version plateaus at a worse level on transfer** ([Wu et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21332)). The two operations run on different statistics. A chunk is found from dependence between neighbours; a class is found from sameness of neighbours. Two members of one class typically do not predict each other at all, because they are alternatives for the same position, and that observation returns in the assessment of rule 2.

One operation does suffice if it carries a variable. Anti-unification keeps the structure two expressions share and puts a variable where they differ ([Anti-unification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-unification)). Library learners such as DreamCoder and Stitch apply that step at scale to programs, so that, on the notes' reading, the fixed body of a new function is a chunk and the range of its parameter is a class ([Ellis et al. 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381); [Bowers et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.16605)). Wolff's SP theory makes the same claim for pattern alignment, while stating that its implemented model does not yet learn intermediate levels ([Wolff 2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.3888)). This is the strongest formal support for the designer's guess, and it carries a price and a caveat. The price is a representation with binding. The caveat is that a variable with a range is a disjunction, so the two operations have moved inside the single step; and since one combinator with application is already computationally complete ([Combinatory logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatory_logic)), the bare statement that one recursive operation suffices is true and uninformative. Minimum description length unifies the objective under which both operations are scored, not the operations. The exact formal link between them is an adjunction: in the lattice of languages, concatenation and its residuals satisfy x·y ≤ z iff y ≤ x\z, so a category can be defined as whatever, placed after x, yields a z ([Residuated lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Residuated_lattice); [Clark 2015](https://doi.org/10.1093/logcom/ext037)). Sequence and category determine each other without being the same operation.

The same two-constructor shape appears wherever hierarchies with both parts and kinds are formalised. And-Or graphs, sum-product networks and algebraic data types are least fixed points of a sum-of-products construction, one recursion scheme iterating a functor with two kinds of node ([Zhu & Mumford 2006](https://doi.org/10.1561/0600000018); [Poon & Domingos 2011](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.3732); [Initial algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_algebra)). Merge, the single recursive operation of minimalist syntax, covers the product side only, and the categories it combines come from the lexicon ([Hauser, Chomsky & Fitch 2002](https://chomsky.info/wp-content/uploads/20021122.pdf); [Merge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merge_%28linguistics%29)). On whether parts-within-wholes and kinds-over-instances can be collapsed into one order, the notes found **no impossibility theorem**, and say so. Both are partial orders. What separates them is that a whole is a conjunction of its parts while a kind is a disjunction of its sub-kinds, that a more specific concept gains attributes while a part inherits nothing from its whole ([Formal concept analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_concept_analysis)), and that restricted senses of parthood fail transitivity where kind-of does not ([SEP, Mereology](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mereology/)). One representational format can hold both as two edge types. A single edge type forgets which nodes are conjunctive.

Where formal theory does define a concept by prediction, the defining relation is not the one rule 2 uses. The states of a minimal automaton are classes of histories with identical sets of continuations ([Myhill–Nerode theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myhill%E2%80%93Nerode_theorem)). Causal states are classes of histories with identical distributions over futures, and they are the unique minimal sufficient statistic for prediction ([Shalizi & Crutchfield 2001](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9907176)). Bisimulation classes are states with equal rewards and equal transition probabilities into each class ([Ferns, Panangaden & Precup 2004](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.4114)). In every case two items are equivalent because they predict the same things, not because they predict each other. Crutchfield's hierarchical reconstruction re-applies this grouping level by level, and his own account is that each jump also needs a structural element whose discovery is not formalised; his first level, grouping consecutive measurements into recurring subsequences, is chunking, and every later level groups states into classes ([Crutchfield 1994](https://csc.ucdavis.edu/~cmg/papers/CalcEmerg.pdf)). He supplies a usable promotion trigger: go up a level when model size at the current level diverges as accuracy increases.

## Brains keep serial order and conceptual order in separable codes

### Learned lists are item-to-position bindings, not successor chains

Simple chaining is the settled casualty. In human immediate recall a confusable item does not raise errors on the item after it, which is the pattern chaining predicts ([Henson et al. 1996](https://doi.org/10.1080/713755612)). Rhesus monkeys that had learned four lists acquired a new list built from one item of each almost without error when each item kept its old ordinal position, and found it as hard as a fresh list when positions changed, although no adjacent pair had ever been trained ([Chen, Swartz & Terrace 1997](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.1997.tb00687.x)). After four 7-item lists, monkeys ordered pairs drawn from different lists correctly **91%** of the time on first presentation ([Terrace, Son & Brannon 2003](https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9280.01420)), and pigeons show the same derived-list advantage ([Scarf & Colombo 2011](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0023695)). The neural code matches. Macaque prefrontal delay activity decomposes into one low-dimensional subspace per ordinal rank, reusable across sequences ([Xie et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abm0204)), and human replay runs an abstract position code that leads the item code by about 50 ms ([Liu et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6657653/)). Order is represented apart from the things ordered. What remains contested is the format of that code (a spatial line, a rank category or an emergent conjunction), not its existence.

This matters for conceptual order because of a recent analytical result. Any learner that codes the two items of a pair additively necessarily implements a ranking and generalises transitively; **a fully conjunctive code, one unit per pair, cannot** ([Lippl et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11252811/)). A store of successor links is the conjunctive extreme. The representation that recalls the next item and the representation that supports inference over an order are therefore different representations, even when one learning rule trains both. Transitive inference from adjacent pairs is established in every vertebrate tested, but its mechanism is contested: value-transfer accounts fail several monkey tests and no single model covers all the data ([Jensen, Terrace & Ferrera 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6710392/)).

The strongest behavioural evidence in the designer's favour sits here. Humans who learned two sets of objects from adjacent comparisons formed a one-dimensional rank line in parietal and dorsomedial prefrontal cortex, and after only 20 trials on a single linking pair the two lines fused into one in the better performers ([Nelli et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10618408/)). EEG patterns for six images ranked by learned reward aligned with the patterns for the digits 1 to 6 ([Luyckx et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6405242/)). Monkeys carried one list representation between a sequence-production task and a pairwise inference task ([Jensen et al. 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3729468)). A learned sequence and an ordered scale share a format, item-to-rank binding comparable by distance. They do not share successor links.

### The count list scaffolds number without becoming it

Number is the test case the designer's own example invokes, and development separates what the hypothesis joins. Children recite the count words in order from about age two with no numerical meaning, become one-knowers around 2;6, and acquire "two", "three" and sometimes "four" one at a time over the following months before counting is used to produce any set ([Odic, Le Corre & Halberda 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2015.01.008)). Among 73 children who could nearly all count to ten, only cardinal-principle knowers understood that adding one item means moving exactly one word along the list ([Sarnecka & Carey 2008](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/8dh3972h)). Whether that transition is an induction of the successor function over the list is contested, and the later evidence runs against it. Many cardinal-principle knowers do not know which of two counted numbers is larger ([Davidson, Eng & Barner 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22245033/)); meaning arrives before order ([Spaepen et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2018.06.012)); successor knowledge generalises only at about 5.5 to 6 years ([Chu et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1111/cogs.12875)); and among Tsimane' children counting mastery proved neither necessary nor sufficient for the logic of exact equality ([Jara-Ettinger et al. 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5319921/)).

The one formal model in which recursion is the decisive step treats the ordered list as a given primitive. Its recursive procedure peels one element off a set and steps one word along the list, its authors insist the procedure is not a successor function, and explicit successor knowledge is left as a further step. The model does predict that a child who recited the number words in some other order would not make the transition, which is the clearest formal statement in this literature of the list as a necessary scaffold ([Piantadosi, Tenenbaum & Goodman 2012](https://web.stanford.edu/~ngoodman/papers/COGNIT2374.pdf)). A 2025 rival derives exact number from repetition, concatenation and nesting applied to sets, with +1 as one simple expression among many and not the foundation ([Dehaene, Sablé-Meyer & Ciccione 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7618345/)).

Adult imaging keeps the split. Judging whether digits are in order and judging which digit is greater share **1.1%** of activated voxels, below chance, whereas the same two judgments on dot arrays share **40.2%**; digit ordering engages premotor cortex and shows a reversed distance effect, the signature of rote list retrieval ([Lyons & Beilock 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6618433/)). A monkey could not learn the non-monotonic order 3-1-4-2 over numerosities across 13 stimulus sets, though arbitrary photographs are learned in any order ([Terrace 2010](https://comparative-cognition-and-behavior-reviews.org/vol5_terrace/)). Magnitude order is intrinsic to its representation; list order is imposed. What does not dissociate as cleanly is numerical and non-numerical order: comparing letters and comparing numbers co-activate anterior intraparietal sulcus ([Fias et al. 2007](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6672167/)). The supported shared component is order over items, not order as magnitude. Single-patient dissociations between the rote sequence and cardinal meaning are reported in both directions, but the notes give those cases only from secondary descriptions or memory, so they are unverified here ([Rubinsten & Sury 2011](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0024079)). The one counter-case is a parrot. Alex, taught labels and ordinal position for the numerals 7 and 8, went on to label sets of seven and eight objects untrained, an inference from list position to cardinal value that rests on a single animal, came only after his earlier numerals already had cardinal meanings, and was not made by two chimpanzees ([Pepperberg & Carey 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3434310/)).

### Sequence statistics yield maps and clusters in hippocampus, not taxonomies

Whether the machinery is shared depends on which of four senses is meant: the same region, the same circuit motif, the same computation, or analogy. The review closest to the designer's question argues for at least five distinct sequence mechanisms (transitions and timing, chunking, ordinal knowledge, algebraic patterns, nested trees). The first three are shared with monkeys and need no recursion, and the fifth requires a recursive neural code its authors describe as not yet identified ([Dehaene et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2015.09.019)). A unified recursive mechanism would on that account cover only the top of the ladder.

The same-region claim fails at Broca's area. Individually localised language regions do not respond to music, arithmetic, logic, code or action observation ([Fedorenko, Ivanova & Regev 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41583-024-00802-4)); nested geometric sequences drove dorsal area 44 while language voxels stayed silent ([Wang et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6592816)); and the only direct test of generating a new hierarchical level in a motor sequence found no inferior frontal involvement ([Martins et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.24549)). The localiser method behind the selectivity findings is itself disputed as circular ([Drijvers, Small & Skipper 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41583-024-00903-0)). The same-region claim holds in the hippocampal formation. After exposure to objects ordered by a random walk on a hidden graph, entorhinal adaptation the next day scaled with graph distance, with no awareness of structure ([Garvert, Dolan & Behrens 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5407855)). Human hippocampal and entorhinal neurons came to mirror the graph that generated an image sequence ([Tacikowski et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07973-1)). A grid-like signal appeared in entorhinal cortex when people navigated a two-dimensional space of bird shapes ([Constantinescu, O'Reilly & Behrens 2016](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaf0941)). Outside the hippocampus, items from one temporal community evoked more similar patterns in left inferior frontal gyrus when transition probabilities were uniform and only community structure could guide parsing ([Schapiro et al. 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3749823)). This is the best empirical case for one mechanism serving sequence and relational structure.

Its limit is exact. These studies show which items belong together and how far apart they are. **None shows a multi-level taxonomy or a part-whole hierarchy arising in neural data from sequence exposure.** The demonstrations that prediction yields a category tree are simulations: Elman's recurrent network, whose clusters its author called soft and implicit, and the Tolman-Eichenbaum Machine trained on family trees ([Elman 1990](https://papers.baulab.info/papers/Elman-1990.pdf); [Whittington et al. 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7707106)). Long-term conceptual knowledge also appears to live elsewhere. The notes recall, without having re-read the papers, that children with early bilateral hippocampal damage reach near-normal factual knowledge and that semantic dementia erodes concepts while sparing episodic and procedural memory ([Vargha-Khadem et al. 1997](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.277.5324.376); [Patterson, Nestor & Rogers 2007](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn2277)); both are unverified. In the basal ganglia, start and stop signals that bracket a learned action sequence are established, with the share of striatal neurons carrying them rising from about 16% to 28% over training ([Jin & Costa 2010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3477867)). Whether the striatum orders sequences at all is disputed, since inactivating its motor output left overlearned sequences fluent ([Desmurget & Turner 2010](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0163-10.2010)), and the extension to syntax rests on one training-transfer study that awaits replication ([Thibault et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abe0874)). Dissociations rule out a single substrate and are silent on shared computation: one algorithm in separately lesionable modules fits all of it. The largest gap relative to the designer's question is that the sequence-learning literature contains almost nothing on part-whole or kind-instance structure as such.

## Thought replaces behavior without symbols; symbols let thought act on thought

The third part of the guess has a precise ancestor. Hull described how a chain of responses, each conditioned to the stimulus trace of the one before, comes to run without the world sequence that trained it, faster than that sequence, with its intermediate links kept only as cues for later links and shrunk toward a purely central trace. He named those cue-only links pure stimulus acts and presented them as the organic basis of symbolism ([Hull 1930](https://www.appstate.edu/~steelekm/classes/psy5300/Documents/Hull1930.pdf)). Two features of his account bear on the design. His symbol is any act-derived internal event that works only as a stimulus, with no arbitrariness and no compositionality, and he declined to explain verbal symbols. His second mechanism, short-circuiting under a persistent goal stimulus, deletes the intermediate sequence and keeps the answer. It explains automatisation, which is the reverse of building a unit that can be operated on.

The modern evidence establishes the first mechanism and removes symbols from it. Hippocampal ensembles in rats sweep ahead of the animal down one arm and then the other at a choice point ([Johnson & Redish 2007](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6673267)) and pre-play the path to a remembered goal from novel starting points ([Pfeiffer & Foster 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3990408)). The behaviour appears when the animal knows the task but the choice is hard, and fades as the policy becomes automatic ([Redish 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5029271)). Agents such as MuZero and DreamerV3 plan by rollout in a learned latent model with no symbolic representation supplied ([Schrittwieser et al. 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08265); [Hafner et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04104)), and feeding a language model's hidden state back as its next input beats verbalised reasoning on search-heavy tasks ([Hao et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06769)). Whether awake replay is planning or memory maintenance is contested ([Gillespie et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8497431)). The strong identifications of thought with covert speech fail. Thinking survives full curare paralysis, a 1947 result reported through [Hesslow 2002](https://www.cs.helsinki.fi/u/ahyvarin/teaching/niseminar4/Hesslow_Simulation.pdf); people with global aphasia retain arithmetic and reasoning ([Fedorenko & Varley 2016](https://doi.org/10.1111/nyas.13046)); and a 2026 study, which the researchers read only as abstract and press account, reports intact rule induction in two patients with severe aphasia and a language network that stays silent during logic ([Kean et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2520095123)). If symbols matter, they are not natural-language words.

The notes sort what remains into three mechanisms that are usually run together. Anticipatory rollout, a sequence predictor run with output gated off and its prediction fed back as input, is strongly supported and needs no symbols. Compilation, the caching of rollout results so that the sequence drops out, is strongly supported and is the opposite of abstraction. Re-representation, giving a mastered sequence or the coordination among several a token that the same machinery can take as an element, is the only place where one recursive mechanism for sequence learning and conceptual order is actually claimed. Its clearest advocate is Piaget. For him an operation is an interiorised, reversible action that exists only inside a structure; classes and series are drawn from the subject's own actions of grouping and ordering; each level is rebuilt by projecting the coordinations of the level below onto a higher plane; and logic does not derive from language ([Piaget 1964](https://people.wou.edu/~girodm/611/Piaget.pdf); [von Glasersfeld 1991](https://dbis-digivis.uibk.ac.at/mediawiki/index.php?oldid=3239)). Its clearest critic is Karmiloff-Smith, for whom redescription of a mastered procedure into an explicit format is a process distinct from the implicit learning that produced it, recursive and triggered by success. McClelland's assessment is that networks model the initial learning well and do not yet perform the redescription endogenously ([McClelland 2021](https://stanford.edu/~jlmcc/papers/McClelland21AppreciationOfAKS.pdf)). Inner speech fits a tool reading: articulatory suppression hurts task switching only when cues are opaque and leaves matrix reasoning untouched ([Alderson-Day & Fernyhough 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4538954)).

The phrase in the hypothesis therefore conflates two things the literature keeps apart. A symbol as cue, a re-enterable internal event, is all that thought-replacing-behavior requires, and animals have it. A symbol as handle (a compact token that makes a procedure an operand), as system (reference fixed by relations among tokens, as in [Harnad 1990](https://www.southampton.ac.uk/~harnad/Papers/Harnad/harnad90.sgproblem.html)), or as loop (tokens that refer to the system's own operations) concerns building higher-order structure. The reverse dependency is also defended: Corballis holds that recursion arose in mental time travel before language and that language evolved to share it, and a reviewer objects that the comparative evidence for that priority is absent ([Corballis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3812905/); [DeWitt 2013](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2013.00812)). The data do not decide between symbols enabling recursion and recursion enabling symbols. The notes found no experiment that manipulates the availability of an arbitrary internal token and measures its effect on the formation of higher-order units.

## Rule 1: promotion by recurrence builds wholes and needs a partner to build kinds

Rule 1 is chunking, and as chunking it stands on firm ground. Cortico-striatal circuits come to mark the first and last elements of a learned action sequence ([Jin & Costa 2010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3477867)). Chunk-and-pass theory holds that language input must be recoded into chunks at successively more abstract levels because it is otherwise overwritten ([Christiansen & Chater 2016](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X1500031X)). The closest published implementation of the rule as stated is the 2009 hierarchical temporal memory node, which memorises coincidence patterns, partitions their transition matrix into Markov chains, and sends its parent one value per chain, so the parent sees sequence identities and not individual patterns ([George & Hawkins 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2749218)). The evidence behind that implementation is thin. The later, biologically detailed model is a single layer with no hierarchy and no upward naming ([Hawkins & Ahmad 2016](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neural-circuits/articles/10.3389/fncir.2016.00023/full)), the researchers located no peer-reviewed evaluation of the pooling step that would turn a sequence into a stable name, and the group has since moved from temporal sequences to location-based reference frames ([Hawkins et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6336927)). Where one rule is applied recursively and results are published, the scale is small: a 22-symbol task, 29 words, 15-node graphs.

Five findings constrain the rule. First, what it builds is partonomy. Chunk-only learners produce part-whole hierarchies and do not transfer to new items ([Wu et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21332)); kinds over instances require the second, context-sharing operation, and a promotion rule keyed on recurrence will not supply it. Second, contiguity cannot tell two views of one thing from two things that reliably follow each other. Swapping one object for another across a change of retinal position altered the position tolerance of inferotemporal neurons within about an hour, so that different objects came to be treated as the same ([Li & DiCarlo 2008](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/1641475)). A promoted unit is an association cluster unless something else constrains it. Third, the criterion for promotion is a real choice. Schmidhuber's history compressor, the cleanest recursive level-builder on record, passes upward what a level failed to predict, and it solved a 20-step-lag task in under 5,000 sequences in 13 of 17 runs where a conventional recurrent network showed no significant improvement after more than a million ([Schmidhuber 1992](https://www.gwern.net/doc/ai/nn/rnn/1992-schmidhuber.pdf)). Promoting surprises builds a hierarchy of event boundaries, promoting recurrences builds a hierarchy of chunks, and Crutchfield's divergence criterion is a third option. Fourth, levels need separated timescales and separated learning rates. In a multiple-timescale recurrent network, fast units learned reusable primitives and slow units their ordering, and with equal time constants performance was significantly worse ([Yamashita & Tani 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2570613/)). A hippocampal model used a tenfold difference in learning rate between the pathway that stores specifics and the pathway that extracts regularities ([Schapiro et al. 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5124075)), and complementary learning systems theory assigns the two jobs to separate systems ([Kumaran, Hassabis & McClelland 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2016.05.004)); the notes read that theory as predicting interference when a promoter shares weights and rates with the fast sequence store. Fifth, community-level abstraction can arise with no promotion step at all. Imprecise temporal integration of transitions blurs a learned transition matrix until cluster structure dominates, and the resulting model predicts human reaction times ([Lynn et al. 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.12491)). That gives one level only; nothing in it recurses.

Two further cautions come from outside chunking models. Sequence context must sometimes split a unit before any merging is meaningful. Clone-structured graphs assign different latent states to the same observation in different contexts and obtain hierarchy from a separate community-detection pass ([George et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8062558/)). In hierarchical reinforcement learning, likewise, discovering the levels is treated as a different computation from learning within them, and the reported direction of dependence runs from clusters of states to chunks of action ([Botvinick, Niv & Barto 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2783353/); [Tomov et al. 2020](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1007594)). And a promoted unit serves conceptual order only if its parts stay addressable. Karmiloff-Smith's account implies that a compiled sequence runs as a whole until a separate consolidation opens it, and Piaget's test of a genuine higher-order unit is reversibility and closure under composition, not replay. The species data point the same way: in a spatial sequence task only humans spontaneously compressed sequences by chunking, while monkeys were fit by a conjunctive rank-by-item code even after extensive training ([Zhang et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0603-21.2021)). The rule should stay, with a significance test in place of raw recurrence (ADIOS and the hierarchical variable model both use one), a paired class-forming rule keyed on shared context, a gap in timescale and learning rate between levels, and constituents that remain readable after promotion.

## Rule 2: a tight loop is a composite until shared context makes it a class

### The rule is the preorder quotient, with three conditions attached

Rule 2 has an exact formal counterpart. For any reflexive, transitive relation, defining a ~ b when a ≤ b and b ≤ a gives an equivalence relation whose quotient is a partial order, and a preorder is the same data as a partition plus an order on the blocks ([nLab, preorder](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/preorder); [Preorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preorder)). In graph terms the rule is condensation by strongly connected components ([Strongly connected component](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strongly_connected_component)). Mereology contains it as a recognised option. Classical parthood is antisymmetric by axiom, so mutual parts are identical; non-extensional systems drop antisymmetry, and Cotnoir's abstract states that making the resulting mereological equivalence explicit recovers classical structure ([SEP, Mereology](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mereology/); [Cotnoir 2010](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9213.2009.649.x)). The designer's wording is defensible there: under the stricter of the two standard definitions of proper part, distinct mutual parts are parts that are not proper parts of each other. The notes add that numbered levels presuppose this step, because proper parthood that admits loops, as in [Cotnoir & Bacon 2012](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1755020311000293), cannot assign ranks.

Three formal conditions attach. The construction needs transitivity, so the test must be mutual reachability and not a direct mutual link. A rule that fires on direct two-way links finds 2-cycles, and the word "two", the digit 2 and an intensity of 2 would join one class only if every pair were trained. The operation is idempotent, so applied again it does nothing unless a new relation is defined over the classes; rule 2 cannot itself generate successive levels. And the meaning of the class depends wholly on the base relation. Mutual succession yields a cycle, the communicating class of a Markov chain, whose members are not interchangeable ([Markov chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_chain)); mutual substitutability yields a category; mutual parthood yields coincidence. Philosophy offers three resolutions of the coincidence case: identity, which merges the nodes with no new level; coincidence, which records the equivalence one level up; and a further formal component, which denies the symmetry and makes the statue the clay plus a form ([SEP, Material Constitution](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/material-constitution/)). The view that statue and clay are mutual parts is reported as unpopular among philosophers ([SEP, Mereology](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mereology/)). The third resolution is a design alternative worth holding open, since an apparent loop can signal a missing component.

### Two-way training builds a resonance, and the reverse link belongs to the learner

What two-way serial training builds in a network is a resonance, not a new unit. A bidirectional associative memory that stores both directions in one matrix settles into a bidirectionally stable pair, a fixed point and not a sequence ([Kosko 1988](https://doi.org/10.1109/21.87054)). An attractor network modified to store a fixed sequence yields attractors correlated with several neighbouring patterns, significant out to a separation of five ([Griniasty, Tsodyks & Amit 1993](https://mlanthology.org/neco/1993/griniasty1993neco-conversion)); the notes recall, without re-checking, that the added coupling is symmetric between neighbours. Cortex looks the same. Pair-coding neurons in anterior temporal cortex respond to both members of a learned pair ([Sakai & Miyashita 1991](https://www.nature.com/articles/354152a0.pdf)), and cell-assembly theory binds word form and referent into one reciprocally connected circuit with no extra symbolic node ([Pulvermüller, Garagnani & Wennekers 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4228116)). All of these are graded similarity in a fixed population. Allocating a unit one level up takes a threshold and an allocation step that the models do not contain. Timing decides which structure a single plasticity rule produces: co-activity that is long relative to the plasticity delay strengthens reciprocal links and gives an assembly, while rapid succession strengthens one-way links and gives a chain ([Pereira & Brunel 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6978734); [Clopath et al. 2010](https://www.nature.com/articles/nn.2479)). Serial presentation in both directions is thus the harder route to a symmetric link. Overlapping presentation is the easier one, and simultaneous presentation of two cues gives stronger sensory preconditioning than serial presentation ([Honey & Dwyer](https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/147851/3/Honey.%20Higher-order%20conditioning%20DOI%20%281%29.pdf)).

The behavioural record says the reverse link is a property of the learner, not a product of the second direction of training. In adult humans it is present after one-way exposure. After three days of passive viewing of pairs in a single order, 55 adults showed violation responses for the reversed order across a frontoparietal and insular network with no interaction with direction, evidence that the journal's assessment rated solid, against incomplete for the four macaques tested ([van Kerkoerle et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11820117/)). A word pair studied once is recalled about equally well from either end, and forward and backward recall of the same pair are highly correlated, the signature of one holistic representation; the exact statistics were not retrieved ([Kahana 2002](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/BF03195769); [Popov et al. 2019](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.3758/s13421-019-00950-4.pdf)). The infant version of the claim is contested, resting on one experiment with a corrected p of 0.044 and a disputed design ([Kabdebon & Dehaene-Lambertz 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6431210); [Chartier & Rey 2020](https://www.pubmed.gov/31942690/)).

Outside humans, 24 studies of emergent symmetry found strong evidence in 3 ([Lionello-DeNolf 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2685068)). **Explicit two-way training did not close the gap.** Two language-trained chimpanzees with long bidirectional lexigram histories failed symmetry tests even when correct test responses were reinforced ([Dugdale & Lowe 2000](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1284759)), and pigeons given alternating forward and backward training showed no bidirectional association where humans benefited, a result the notes take from an abstract only ([Navarro & Wasserman 2020](https://iro.uiowa.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Bidirectional-conditioning-Revisiting-Asratyans-alternating-training/9984070259702771)). Identical forward training can even yield the opposite of symmetry when the baseline relations change ([Urcuioli 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2582203)), and in Pavlovian preparations few backward pairings give excitation while many give inhibition ([Chang, Blaisdell & Miller 2003](https://pigeonrat.psych.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/144/2019/09/Chang-Blaisdell-Miller-2003-JEPABP.pdf)). Multiple-exemplar training in both directions did install generalised symmetry in one sea lion and, at abstract level, in one infant ([Schusterman & Kastak 1993](https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1007/BF03395915); [Luciano et al. 2007](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1868588)), and on that reading bidirectionality is a skill of a trained system applied to new pairs, not something that falls out of each pairing. Next-token language models behave like the pigeons: fine-tuned on a fact stated in one direction, they assign the correct answer in the reverse direction no more likelihood than a random name, and GPT-4 answered a parent-of-celebrity question 79% of the time forward and 33% in reverse ([Berglund et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.12288)). Training on reversed text mitigates the effect, though one analysis reports that neither reversed tokens nor naive bidirectional attention removes it ([Golovneva et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13799v3); [Kitouni et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05183)). For a directional learner, presenting both directions is necessary, which supports the designer's procedure. The animal data say it is not sufficient for a class.

### Classes form through common associates, names and responses

Where equivalence classes do form, they are real, graded, and built by something other than mutual prediction. After a few arbitrary conditional discriminations, verbally able humans show untrained symmetry and transitivity, and a shock paired with one class member transferred skin-conductance responses to the others in 6 of 8 subjects, with extinction transferring too ([Dougher et al. 1994](https://contextualscience.org/publications/transfer_respondent_eliciting_extinction_functions_through_stimulus_equivalence)). Relatedness within a class nonetheless falls with the number of trained links between members, so the class is a graded network, and whether that gradient fades once the class has formed is disputed ([Moss-Lourenco & Fields 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3088076)). Order is kept alongside the class: humans rated reversed pairs less familiar than canonical ones ([van Kerkoerle et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11820117/)), and baboons show partial transfer that its authors read as a directional code coexisting with a non-directional one ([Chartier & Fagot 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10272242)).

The routes to class behaviour that the literature documents are many-to-one arrangements: a common associate, since cues that share a training history generalise to each other ([Honey & Hall 1989](https://doi.org/10.1037/0097-7403.15.4.338)); a common name or response ([Miguel 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6381345)); and reinforcement structure across a whole set of relations ([Urcuioli 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2582203)). Whether a shared representation results is contested. Hall's account needs only mediation by the evoked associate and no new unit ([Hall 1996](https://scilit.com/publications/6d1616ac9522b3dc5bccdb3ba2b01c56)); hippocampal and midbrain activity during learning predicted later generalisation ([Shohamy & Wagner 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2628634)); and overlap can also drive representations apart ([Schlichting, Mumford & Preston 2015](https://link.springer.com/10.1038/ncomms9151)). A label's effect on a category is on-line and transient (in one cueing example the benefit fades after about 1600 ms), which argues against a permanent merged unit ([Lupyan 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3297074)). Whether equivalence is a primitive outcome of reinforcement, a product of naming, or a learned relational operant is unresolved among Sidman, Horne and Lowe, and relational frame theory.

One replicated asymmetry speaks to the output-error penalty. In young children, training the speaker direction (see the object, say the name) yielded untrained listener behaviour in all participants across several studies, while listener training yielded naming in only 2 of 9 and 10 of 14 ([Sivaraman & Barnes-Holmes 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10733260/)). The production direction supplies the reverse link. The comprehension direction does not.

### A number word and a magnitude are joined by a third code

For the designer's own example, the literature describes two directed mappings that later cohere, joined by a third representation. Among 62 children aged 2;7 to 4;6, producing roughly the right number of taps for a spoken number word worked for two- and three-knowers, while naming a flashed quantity did not work even for cardinal-principle knowers as a group; the authors conclude the mapping is not functionally bidirectional in early development, with the word-to-magnitude direction established first ([Odic, Le Corre & Halberda 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2015.01.008)). In two monkeys trained for months to associate numerals with dot numerosities, **23% of lateral prefrontal neurons** were tuned to the same value in both formats, against **about 2% in the intraparietal sulcus**, where the magnitude code sits ([Diester & Nieder 2007](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.0050294)). That is the nearest physiological analogue of a meta-concept: a symmetric code located apart from both sign and magnitude. It joins them without replacing either. In human intraparietal cortex, dot numerosities show analogue pattern overlap while digits show a flat, categorical similarity structure, and a digit shares no number-specific pattern with the matching number of dots ([Lyons, Ansari & Beilock 2015](https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.22641)). Whether number symbols are grounded in approximate magnitude at all is contested, and two 2016 reviews conclude they are organised mainly by order relations among symbols ([Reynvoet & Sasanguie 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5061812/); [Leibovich & Ansari 2016](https://doi.org/10.1037/cep0000070)). Children connect the two systems both by item-by-item association and by structure mapping, a holistic alignment of one ordered system to the other ([Sullivan & Barner 2014](https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12211)). The premise that the two members cannot be ordered needs care here. Each already sits in an order of its own, the word in the count list and the intensity on a magnitude scale, and what the class has to do is align two orders.

The implications for rule 2 follow from that ledger. Keep the quotient, because layering needs it, and trigger it on mutual reachability so that classes can have more than two members. Add the meta-concept as a third unit that binds the pair, and leave the members and their directional links in place, since humans keep order alongside equivalence and classes stay graded. Certify a class by transfer of function and shared context, not by closure of the loop: a new association trained on one member should appear on the other, and the two should predict and be predicted by the same third parties. Put the error penalty on the production direction, object to word, and do not expect the comprehension direction to deliver it. Treat each merger as a retractable hypothesis, because inferring full equivalence from partial evidence is provably safe only under restrictions such as substitutability ([Clark & Eyraud 2007](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume8/clark07a/clark07a.pdf)). For graded members such as an intensity, exact classes are brittle, and the developed alternatives are bisimulation metrics and vectors of predictions ([Ferns, Panangaden & Precup 2004](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.4114); [Littman & Sutton 2001](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2001/hash/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Abstract.html)). A concept defined as a closed set under a two-way relation between items and their contexts gives classes and the order among them from a single closure, which is the formal construction closest to kinds over instances ([Formal concept analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_concept_analysis)).

## Conclusion

The designer's guess is right about three things and wrong about one. It is right that the scheme is recursive, that time is the dominant teaching signal for abstraction, and that loops must be resolved before levels can be numbered. It is wrong about the count of operations, and the error is located precisely: as stated, rule 2 fires on the same statistic as rule 1. Mutual prediction is the strongest possible chunk. Changing rule 2's trigger from "each predicts the other" to "each predicts, and is predicted by, the same others" turns the two rules into the alternating compose-and-classify loop that every generalising system in this literature uses, with the word-object loop as the special case in which both conditions hold. The number example adds a second correction: a word and a magnitude are aligned as two ordered scales, so the meta-concept is better built as a mapping between orders than as a per-pair merger. On symbols, the hypothesis should be restated. Covert rollout is old, shared with rats and needs no symbols; the place where one mechanism for sequence and concept is genuinely claimed is re-representation, where a token makes a mastered procedure into an operand, and Piaget's criterion (reversibility and closure under composition) is the test of whether a promoted unit has become a concept or remains a compiled habit.

The literature leaves three experiments unrun, and a layered architecture can run all of them in simulation: one-way versus two-way versus common-associate training compared on transitivity, transfer of function and representational similarity; mutual prediction pitted against shared context on directed transition graphs, which the community-structure studies confound; and the presence or absence of an arbitrary internal token for a mastered sequence, measured by whether its parts become recombinable. Several load-bearing items deserve a page check before the design leans on them: the patient double dissociations in number, the hippocampal and semantic-dementia dissociations, the symmetric form of the Griniasty coupling, and the pigeon two-way training result. The research notes also record that the search budget ran out in five of the six strands, so work from 2025 and 2026 is thinly covered.
