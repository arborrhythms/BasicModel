# `intuit`: the deterministic top-down predictor over STM (Claude, 2026-10-21; for Codex, item 3)

**Current design: §5 (Alec, 2026-10-21, later).** `intuit` is an order-0
parallel calculus, not the ARMA point predictor; §2's history and §3–§4's
ARMA design are superseded and kept for the record.

## 1. Decision (Alec, 2026-10-21)

"Reintroduce [the retired ARMA predictor] as `intuit()`, which takes the
STM vectors and predicts a conceptual vector. It can be called
deterministically; expectation of the conceptual state is more of a
bias/variance predictor, used bottom-up, not top-down." Two predictors,
two contracts: **expectation** (the gain field `c = m⊙a⊙o` over symbol
activations, both lanes) modulates perception and parsing; **`intuit`**
is a point predictor from meaning to meaning that thinking and
production read. This supersedes the FutureWork amendment of the same
day that read `intuit` as the expectation predictor exposed.

**Placement (Alec, 2026-10-21): implemented as part of item 3**, not
item 3.5. Alec accepted the spec ("Great"), read as confirming §3's
move of the three top-down consumers to `intuit`.

## 2. What existed, and what is top-down today

Retired in item 4 (`f7e74af04`): `BracketExpectation`'s ARMA(p, q)
(`f7e74af04^:bin/Layers.py`): a sentence vector `s_t`, AR over the last
`p` (`_s_history`), MA over the last `q` residuals `e_t = s_t − ŝ_t`
(`_e_history`), an MLP `Linear → Tanh → Linear`, loss
`MSE(predictor(s_{t−1..t−p}, e_{t−1..t−q}), s_t)`, defaults `p = 5`,
`q = 2`; and the structured `SentenceExpectation` (three roles plus
masks over a context window). Both were replaced by the shared
`ActivationPredictor` (`Expectation.py`, hidden 64) behind
`SymbolExpectation.expect_next_meaning`.

That forecast is read **top-down today in three places**, so the
separation Alec describes is not yet true in code:
(a) generation's seed, `Models.py` ≈7023 ("the next prediction is a
generation seed"); (b) FUTURE what-answers, `Models.py` ≈9065 (the
argmax of expected activation over symbol keys); (c) the thought LTM
capability `expectation(row)`, `Queries.py` ≈1145. Its bottom-up uses
are the sentence-boundary staging before perception (`Models.py` ≈20587
`predict_next_end_state`, ≈20636 `expect_next_meaning`) for the gain and
the prime, and its own training (`sentence_prediction_cost`,
`_discourse_inter_loss`).

## 3. Design, in code names

**Owner.** `bin/Intuition.py`, class `Intuition(nn.Module)`, one instance
`ConceptualSpace.intuition`. ARMA(p, q) over STM:

- AR input: the stream's last `p` STM vectors, newest last: the concept
  points (`point_of_row`, concept width) of the STM index of recent rows,
  the same occurrences `ReferenceClose.accessible` reads as frames; when
  called inside thinking, the open serial meaning's point is the newest
  entry. There is one STM index: if today it lives only in
  `SymbolExpectation._inter_context`, it moves to `ConceptualSpace` and
  both predictors read it (one owner, no second history).
- MA input: the stream's last `q` residuals, a non-persistent ring
  `[B, q, concept_dim]`, reset with STM at `begin_document`.
- Network: the retired shape, `Linear → Tanh → Linear` to concept width,
  output through the cube chart (sigmoid) and `assert_cube`; no clipping.
- Config under `ConceptualSpace`: `<intuitOrder>` (p, default 5),
  `<intuitResidualOrder>` (q, default 2), `<intuitHidden>`; in
  `model.xsd` and Params.

**Deterministic.** A pure function of the STM vectors and the residual
ring: no sampling, no dropout, no exploration, identical in train and
eval; the same state gives the bit-identical vector, eager and compiled.
Trials (`preview`, `prepare`) never push the ring; only a kept close
does.

**Training.** At the sentence-boundary hook (≈20587), before perception,
stage `ŝ` from the STM as it stands. At the kept close, score it against
the closed sentence's concept point (`clause.point`, the LTM1 row's
slot): squared error into its own registry category `intuition`
(trained; inputs detached, so it trains only `Intuition`'s parameters);
push `e = s − ŝ` (detached) into the ring. The first sentence of a
stream is not scored (zero history still yields a stable-shape
prediction). The keep reads R + A only; `intuition` is excluded, like E.

**Thought operation.** `<thought>` gains
`intuit_argout1 = intuit.thought(intuit_argin1)` in every grammar that
declares a thought section; `Queries.py` gains
`ThoughtExecutorDescriptor('intuit', 'conceptual-prediction',
('description',), Mind.SERIAL, (Mind.SERIAL, Mind.LTM, Mind.BUDGET),
(Mind.SERIAL,), 'intuition', _intuit, evidence_pair=True)`. `_intuit`
reads the STM vectors and the serial meaning's point, runs
`ConceptualSpace.intuition` without gradient, and writes the predicted
vector as a one-slot meaning into the serial slot with evidence
**(0, 0)**: a hypothesis in the tetralemma's neither corner until
`isTrue`, an observation or the keep supplies a lane; never a netted
confidence. One tick of work. Its parameters learn only from §3's
prediction error; the chooser learns when to call it from the keep's
credit, like any operation.

**Top-down consumers move to `intuit` (recommended; Alec to confirm).**
(a) The generation seed unfolds `intuit`'s vector through the output
walk; (b) FUTURE what-answers return `intuit`'s vector; (c) the thought
LTM capability `expectation(row)` is retired in favor of the `intuit`
thought operation. Expectation keeps only its bottom-up roles. Audit:
no top-down caller of `expect_next_meaning` remains.

## 4. Gates

1. Determinism: bit-identical vectors for identical STM and ring, eager
   and compiled; trials leave the ring untouched; one push per kept close.
2. Ownership: `intuition` gradients reach only `Intuition`'s parameters;
   keep decisions unchanged when its loss weight is varied.
3. Capacity sanity, a fixed-budget unseeded check: a period-three stream
   of three fixed concepts is predicted below a stated error that a
   zero-history predictor cannot reach. Not a corpus learning claim.
4. The thought operation: selectable; writes one meaning with evidence
   (0, 0); `isTrue` evaluates it afterwards; work charged; the
   initialization-neutrality certificate holds with the larger menu.
5. Consumers moved per §3 and the audit passes; generation and FUTURE
   tests ported under the kept-semantics rule.
6. Unchanged: both decomposition arms at original seeds and budgets,
   §14, G1–G3, reference close, MTM. One sweep at the landing.

**Not in this step:** a large `intuit` (its size is item 0's
measurement: parameters at matched accuracy, frequency of use as the
missing-operator diagnostic); stochastic variants; `intuit` as a
candidate source for `bind`; any learning claim.

## 5. Revised design (Alec, 2026-10-21, later): one layer of order-0 prediction in the parallel calculus

Alec: "intuit() [is] an order-0 parallel calculus (union, intersection,
negation) that implements one layer of full concept-to-concept
prediction; it will leave a set of activated order-0 concepts that
represent knowing, and are used to create the answer/output." Order-0
concepts are event types (Orders §1–§3): no identity, only overlap and
containment; the calculus is exact and parallel there and stops where
individuation begins.

**The layer (revised the same day after Alec's "S1 R S2" decision).**
Relations are LTM1 rows whose two references point to LTM0 occurrences
(two truths §1, 2026-10-21), so the rules `intuit` composes with are
remembered or stated `S1 → S2` rows (kind: implication; provenance tells
an observed succession from a stated if-then), not a matrix over concept
pairs. One call is one layer of Mamdani inference in the order-0
calculus. Read the knowing field `a` as a vector in the order-0 cube (the
union of its active concepts' codes, each clipped at its activation, per
lane). For each rule row `r = (S1, S2)` with lanes `(t⁺, t⁻)`:

- firing `f⁺ = min(t⁺, coverage)` and `f⁻ = min(t⁻, coverage)`, where
  `coverage = |a ∧ c(S1)| / |c(S1)|` is the meet ratio: how much of
  `S1`'s content is known (graded, so partial matches fire partially);
- contribution: `S2`'s content `c(S2)` clipped at `f⁺` into its own
  lanes, and clipped at `f⁻` into the exchanged lanes (negation is the
  lane exchange, `Queries.negate`, never `1 − x`);
- the new knowing is the union (coordinatewise max) of all
  contributions, per lane, written back as order-0 concept activations
  by each concept's containment in it (the knowing effect's existing
  write).

Nothing is netted: a concept can be active in both lanes (the refine
prompt) or neither (unknown). Any content can predict any content
(full concept-to-concept); the scan is sparse over rules whose `S1`
overlaps the knowing (the store's leaf-code index); deterministic, eager
and compiled alike. Deeper inference is repeated calls, each charged by
the work meter. This is intuition by recall: the knowing cues remembered
antecedents by content and unions their consequents.

**Input and output.** Input: the knowing field as it stands, the order-0
activations of the current STM entries (the open slots and the STM
index's recent rows), in both lanes. Output: the same field after one
layer, written through the existing knowing effect (`AccessibleMind`,
the carrier's `_concept_activations`, the `Subsystem.KNOWING` write that
`compose` and `thought` already hold).

**No parameters; the rules are memory.** At each kept close the store
writes one LTM1 row from the stream's previous LTM0 occurrence to the
new one, its lanes the closing's evidence pair (an explicit negation
fills `t⁻`; absence never does); a stated "if S1 then S2" writes the same
kind of row. Retention and forgetting bound the rules as every row;
minting by recurrence (step 2) later lets recurring successions be
restated between minted concepts, which is consolidation from exemplar
rules to concept rules. Until step 2 lands, rows that reference
occurrences do not exist, so `intuit` is implemented after step 2 within
item 3.

**Gradient.** The layer is computed on the knowing field without
detaching: the field's values come from the differentiable MTM read
(bind plan §10.3, "Gradient through a bind"), and max and min pass
subgradients along their active branches, so an answer's gradient
reaches through `intuit` to the bound values, the live STM values that
produced them, and, by the score function, the bind's choice. The rule
rows are data and receive no gradient.

**Thought operation.** `intuit_argout1 = intuit.thought(intuit_argin1)`
in every grammar with a thought section; descriptor
`ThoughtExecutorDescriptor('intuit', 'order-zero-prediction',
('description',), Mind.KNOWING, (Mind.SERIAL, Mind.KNOWING, Mind.LTM,
Mind.BUDGET), (Mind.KNOWING,), 'intuition', _intuit, evidence_pair=True)`.
It writes no serial meaning: it leaves the knowing set, which later
thought operations (`query`, `isTrue`, `bind`) and the output read.
Work: one record per active literal per stored relation read.

**Answer and output.** Production and answering call `intuit` once before
composing; the answer/output path composes from the knowing set it
leaves: generation's seed (`Models.py` ≈7023) and FUTURE answers (≈9065)
read it instead of expectation's forecast, the generate section's word
choices take it as their content prime, and the thought LTM
`expectation(row)` capability retires. Expectation stays the bottom-up
gain. Individuation and predication of the answer are the serial
grammar's (order 1), as for any sentence.

**Gates.**
1. Exactness: on a small bank the sparse layer equals a dense max–min
   reference bit for bit; negation is involutive; eager and compiled
   agree; trials and reads leave `W` unchanged.
2. Learning by writing: one observed succession of sentences `A → B`
   makes `intuit` from knowing `c(A)` activate `B`'s order-0 concepts at
   the row's degree; knowing half of `A` fires the rule at the meet
   ratio; an explicit negation fills the against lane; absence changes
   nothing.
3. A three-sentence cycle `A → B → C → A`, presented once, is predicted
   exactly by three calls; deterministic, no training budget, no seed.
4. Conflict stays visible: `A → B` and `A → ¬B` both observed give both
   lanes for `B`, never a netted value.
5. Knowing is written through the existing effect; the output reads it;
   no top-down caller of `expect_next_meaning` remains.
6. Gradient: with a supplied content answer read through `intuit`, the
   loss's gradient is nonzero on the live STM value a bind selected and
   zero on a selected LTM0 row's stored content and on minted bank rows;
   the bind's candidate logits receive the score-function credit.
7. Unchanged: both decomposition arms, §14, G1–G3, reference close, MTM.
   One sweep at the landing.

**Not in this step:** rules over LTM2 relations;
learned weights; any learning claim (item 0).

gates stand and spec §4 adds gates 8–14 for round B.
