# Selected meaning and one normal thought controller

**Reopened after review of `288b56b`, September 20.** The 4,630-case receipt
was a valid regression result, but the standalone language model did not
satisfy the three-grammar architecture or natural-wording gate. The corrective
implementation removes it, completes the chooser's bounded semantic/memory
context, exercises actual policy-selected nested descent, retires the remaining
bridge/legacy paths and reconciles the test deletions.

[SelectedMeaning](../SelectedMeaning.md) is the current implementation contract;
[KernelRetirement](../KernelRetirement.md) records individual dispositions and
[Testing](../Testing.md) records both the original and corrective receipts.
The user-owned three-grammar specification remains outside this commit.

The remaining item 1 exit gate is grammar-owned natural wording: forward-parsed
text, a relation-wording holdout, noun/converse/alternate-sense controls, normal
vocabulary generation and recomposition. It cannot be met by a fourth
interpreter or realiser. No completion claim survives the codec's deletion.

Matched-compute, multi-seed causal utility remains item 4 as requested, but
learned utility is explicitly unproven until that evidence passes. A fitted
routing fixture and reader traversal prove mechanisms only. Item 2 does not
start merely because a regression suite is green.
