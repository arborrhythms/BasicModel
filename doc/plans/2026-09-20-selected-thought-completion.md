## Replacement contract and scope

The implementation removes the complete alternate frame controller, its
addressee/testimony mechanism, the tool-user class, the parity selector hooks
and the separate next-idea blend/scorer. No facade survives. Public entry points
and normal resolution share the selected controller, completed-row scope,
ordinary history and the episode work meter. Checked numerical readers and the
reviewed, optional answer-bridge loss remain; neither controls a query episode.

The chooser's zero output layer reproduces the fixed baseline in evaluation at
root and child levels. Each candidate carries a grammatical operation, full
operand assignment and open-role/level context; conclude is a transition.
Root and active roles remain live, candidate values and checked evidence detach,
and no answer target enters the policy input. The one explicit policy term uses
row-local supplied-answer quality minus actual shared work. Real two-row
training verifies optimizer ownership, distinguishable rewards and a nonzero
update. FineWeb provides no such answer reward until item 2 adds residual credit.

The full plan section 4 causal utility comparison across seeds, including
direct-answer and equal-compute controls, remains item 4 under the September 20
scope clarification. Item 1 establishes the replacement and its regression
contracts; it does not establish general learned reasoning utility.

The learned language block has no natural-word anchors. Its training test covers
held-out noun pairs, converse/paraphrase, alternate senses, all three observation
writers, checked execution, generation and recomposition. Explicit nested
semantic folds retain precedence over learned lexical alignment. Set/code/child
answers own their payloads through normal resolution and output; set members
share one bounded word budget.
