## What the toys settle, and what they do not

1. **Use a multiplicative gain with a separate residual.** The clipped
   relative rule meets the specified bounded-content and omission behavior.
   Detach the entire saturation, including the expected-magnitude denominator;
   detaching the residual numerator alone leaks downstream credit. Predictors
   train against detached observations and detached source context. A positive
   gain preserves identity only under the identify-before-attenuate carrier;
   scaling before Euclidean lookup can change the identified key. No default gain is selected from
   these toy scores.
2. **Keep R's whole-observation target.** Even a fully detached gain makes
   omission preferable if the chooser is scored against dimmed content.
   R+A costs must compare the two completed trials to the same original
   observation/outcome. E remains reported and trained separately.
3. **Identify before attenuating.** Positive scalar scaling preserves cosine
   direction, but can change Euclidean identification; at zero it erases the
   identifying vector. Carry identity and activation separately. The raw
   observation and estimate support awareness at g=1 without pretending the
   attenuated content still contains a recoverable vector.
4. **Do not claim noisy-TV rejection or higher-order capture.** Correct
   mean/variance prediction still gives normalized residual approximately 1
   for Bernoulli noise. Marginal statistics cannot detect a change solely in
   transition order. The minimal P statistic remains the decided mechanism;
   richer transition prediction and belief-shift novelty remain proposed.
   The cold-start confidence control in toy 5 is imposed, not a learned gate.

These findings qualify the prose in spec §§4.1, 4.5 and 9. They are recorded
here without overwriting the user's new spec. The identity-before-gain
implementation preserves the intended identity gate; it does not weaken it.
The noisy-TV and higher-order claims need a separate mechanism/measurement
before they can be asserted.

## Implementation sequence after this start

### A. Carrier, ownership and observation timing

Introduce an explicit carrier with raw observed activations, predicted
activations, content activations and two residual signs for **each** evidence
lane. An activation pair `(for,against)` and a residual pair `(excess,omission)`
are different axes. Do not subtract the two evidence lanes before measuring
error: `(1,0)` and `(0,1)` must not become a matched magnitude. One scalar
gain per symbol can use summed absolute lane discrepancy divided by summed
expected magnitude plus epsilon. Identity, order stamp and occurrence address
travel beside activation. An assertion on nonnegative lane/cube content must
not clamp signed coordinates inside existing immutable identity codes.

The attenuation factor is
`1 - g * (1-object_exemption) * confidence * (1-s)`; attention multiplies
the resulting content separately, with exact zero outside the region.
An open object exemption restores gain 1 rather than multiplying content by
zero. Residual reporting does not depend on g or the object exemption.
Deep residuals are scoped to admitted regions, including expected absent
symbols *within* that scope; do not invent omissions over every unvisited
symbol outside it. The shallow field statistic supplies outside salience.

Stage the forecast before its target arrives, from the previous accepted
observation state. A forecast's own prime is not a new observation or an input
to its next prediction. Both paired trials share that prior snapshot and
parameter version. Update running statistics/history once for the kept
external observation, after comparison; speculative trials and generated
thoughts must not double-count external evidence. Preserve batch-row,
document and packed/single isolation and checkpoint replay.

### B. P: shallow statistic and region content

Add checkpointed mean, variance and exposure state per native percept
coordinate. Read normalized residual before updating the statistic. Handle
unobserved coordinates and cold confidence explicitly; padding is not an
absence observation. Epsilon, saturation and update convention need named
configuration and validation; the toy's EMA rate is experimental, not an
authorized production default.

Feed per-coordinate z to the placement reader. Pool only over the actual
attended support for the scalar region content gain. Identify the unmodified
percept first; retain its reference even when content gain is zero. Never
gain `.where`/`.when`, resnap a dimmed prototype, or replace the observed
codebook-learning target with z. Gate with native cosine/Euclidean paths,
zero and positive gains, empty regions, cold confidence and resume parity.

### C. S, R and D: one activation-prediction owner

Use shared weights by default, reporting errors by actual order stamps.
Read causal symbolic activation and conceptual STM; predict both lanes of
active symbol identities. A word-candidate softmax is not sufficient for
simultaneous symbols and both evidence lanes: multiple activations need not
sum to one. A shared per-symbol scorer can avoid an N-by-N parameter matrix;
its memory/work bound and active-bank coverage must be measured. Native
handles address rows; their integer values are not learned features.

Freeze forecast identity alignment. A subsequently minted symbol starts
without a prior prediction; a forgotten or reused physical slot must not
inherit a previous handle's forecast. Expected-but-absent admitted symbols
remain in the residual domain. Keep prediction snapshots reachable through
Item 5 compaction and define checkpoint migration before retiring old heads.

Read this owner at symbol entry, sentence closing and next-row forecasting.
Compose has no derived or independent expectation; inversion remains Item 3.
Write expected activation into a separate prime input for chooser/mask,
never into raw comprehension evidence. Thought/answer content consumers
read attenuated whole plus an explicit route to the estimate; salience,
retention and local loss read residuals. Keep raw o in storage. Migrate
generation and anticipatory-query callers explicitly rather than keeping a
hidden structured predictor for them.

`non`'s withheld dimensions must suppress both the matching prior prime and
attenuation while preserving the withheld mark for reconstruction. Map those
dimensions to the new symbol/evidence carrier explicitly; do not exempt an
entire cat because a single modifier dimension is withheld. This contract
is not exercised by the simple object-mask toy.

### D. One departure, one common sentence-end judge

Follow rev 5's R+A judge for both selection and credit. Preserve strict ties
favoring greedy. Change the scorer, comparison audit and reader-weight callers
together, with an answer-versus-R conflict regression; do not change only
the displayed totals. Preserve each parameter's objective ownership.

Draw the alternative from `phi * uniform + (1-phi) * policy_at_zero_gain`,
excluding greedy and respecting eligible actions, with no draw when fewer
than two choices exist. Use the actual mixture probability in any proposal
correction. Where to depart stays uniform over eligible levels then sites;
there is one departure per sentence and a greedy suffix.

Zeroing content attenuation alone is **not** an expectation-free policy if
the positive expectation prime remains an input. The alternative view must
also omit the forecast prime and other prediction-derived chooser features,
while retaining observations, legitimate desire and the same candidate set.
Do not alter the shared observation snapshot or make a second random
derivation. Test the uniform probability floor at extreme logits and the
absence of forecast influence on the alternative distribution.
