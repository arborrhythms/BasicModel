## Decided theory (read before changing anything)

- [doc/Orders.md](../Orders.md): orders are fuzzy sets of symbols; intension
  at order 0; membership by evidence per set; identity per order; two ladders
  (forms, referents), one fold; distributional for words, semantic for
  objects; grammar operations are order operations; a determiner lowers a
  description (type at 0, kind face 2) to a member, order 1 by construction;
  `the` with nothing to bind stays unbound (accommodation).
- Plan [§14.1](2026-10-10-item-6-stored-idea-generativity.md): mint and bind
  are two operators with fixed semantics (indefinite = new by assertion,
  definite = old by presupposition); no operator is tied to a word (UG);
  identity is revisable while the entities are accessible.

  individual at its order reduction with the description as signature;
  matching is a separate step; VPs are named operations whose vectors are

  difference in the order-0 code "however learned", not literally over
  percepts (the "water froze / became ice" example in Orders.md §7).

  = both lanes zero and the prior withheld on that dimension (a per-dimension
