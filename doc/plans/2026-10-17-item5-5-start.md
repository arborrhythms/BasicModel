# Item 5.5 start: occurrence, constituents and verb phases

Item 6 is accepted as a mechanism. This start records the remaining code and
its dependencies; it makes no learning claim and changes no production
mechanism. [Orders](../Orders.md) and the item-6 plan's
[§15–16 decisions](2026-10-10-item-6-stored-idea-generativity.md#15-identity-without-a-mixing-matrix-alec-2026-10-16)
govern the older [5.5 spec](../specs/2026-09-30-occurrence-tense-aspect.md).
In particular, `.when` remains the source-relative interval, mint and bind
remain distinct, and generic descriptions are not lowered merely because a
word is plural or follows `every`.

## First scoped work: stage 4e

Use the stage 4b–4d vocabulary and constructions with generated constituent
spans and teacher trees. Keep text, training annotations and held-out grading
labels separate. Include one- and two-NP clauses, adjective extensions,
transitive VPs and prepositional attachments, with the same word forms already
presented to the learner. Counterbalance the lexical combinations and NP roles;
do not install word→operator anchors or a production ordering rule.

The measurement must report free held-out bracket recovery, NP occurrence
coverage and duplicate writes, actual reference orders, closed meaning norms,
and kept programs. A singular NP counts at its order reduction, and its stored
signature is the description before the sentence fold. Order-2 descriptions
which are not lowered have no order-1 occurrence. Supplied teacher readings
are mechanism diagnostics, not passing free-reading results.

Build and check that corpus/grader before training. Then connect its explicit
annotation targets to the existing compose chooser's CE under paired
explore/keep training. Declare the fixed presentation budget and held-out gate
before the run; keep the established warm-up seed and presentation budgets.
Stop at the constituent gate if held-out brackets or occurrence coverage fail.
There is **no native identity run until stage 4e passes**.

After that gate, the carried sequence is identity matching and document-close
row pairs, VP temporal-difference teaching, direct-operand word classes, and
later order-2 kinds. Neither order-2 design is selected here. The 5.5 mechanism
work retains the spec's clock-independence, offset-independence, addressed-chain,
interleaving, preposition and surface-inverse certificates; the existing item-6
learning failures remain named in todo item 0.

## Stage 4e authorization and fixed measurement (2026-10-17)

Stage 4e runs alone, with one receipt. Its gates, declared before training:

| Question | Gate on the free held-out reading |
|---|---|
| Brackets | Exact constituent brackets on at least 90% of sentences of the trained kinds |
| Singular NPs | Exactly one written occurrence for at least 95% of NPs, with zero duplicate writes |
| Lowering | An actual 2→1 stamp transition at every lowered NP; missing NPs remain failures |
| Unlowered descriptions | No order-1 occurrence |

The fixed budget is **144 training sentence presentations**: 72 generated
sentences, two passes, batches of four, seed **20261010**, learning rate
**.003**. Start from the accepted paired stages 1–2 checkpoint
`output/item6-orders-paired-accepted-code-20261015/warmup.ckpt` (76 prior
presentations, 20/24 singleton gate). No further warm-up, adaptive repeats or
identity presentations. Evaluate once on **32 held-out constituent sentences
and eight unlowered-description controls**. The four trained constructions
are adjective NPs, intransitive clauses, transitive clauses and prepositional
clauses; noun combinations and roles cross between training and evaluation.
New function/relational word forms are encountered in this stated budget;
there is no morphology lesson. The generator, training annotations and grading
labels are separate, and held-out labels never enter a model call.

The lesson supplies explicit compose CE for both folds and waits. The existing
source-reading lesson forces its annotated order, so it is not used here.
Attention and compose both retain their student's choices. Teacher states are
evaluated only after the student's trial has
finished. They do not overwrite its actions, values, order stamps, references
or kept program. The grader reads that actual program and the native writer.
It must not substitute a teacher reading or assign a noun an order by name.
Stop at any failed gate; no native identity run follows a failure.

After this receipt, the separate 5.5 mechanism round retires the legacy clock
timestamp and `Clause.eternal`, replaces global recency with the row's
source-addressed chain, and replaces the English form analyzer with four
learned surface transformations and their inverses. Its measurement is the
spec's clock-independence, offset-independence, addressed-chain, interleaving,
preposition and surface-inverse certificates. Tense/aspect on VP phases waits
for the carried identity sequence. These mechanism changes are not part of
the stage 4e pass.
