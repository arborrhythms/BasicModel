## What a word needs to remember

Alec's intended learned association is between a **word concept and an object concept**. In reverse, an object with a selected name leads to its word concept. The word concept leads through the perceptual whole and its ordered children to the original bytes. For example, the two covers `sho | pping` and `shop | ping` both reconstruct `shopping`, including both occurrences of `p`. These cuts illustrate a possible reading. In the paced lesson the model chooses its own fixation from observed bytes. [Approved sentence and cover decisions](2026-11-24-item1-5-device-sentence.md)

Creating a perceptual whole from observed parts, counting its recurrence, nominating a word concept in MTM, and associating that word with an object are distinct events. The ordered children already determine the spelling. A second learned association from the entire byte string to the word concept would duplicate information. A character property or a containment relation can carry evidence that the ordered children alone do not supply.

## Perceptual-parts experiment and rollback

**Motivation.** The observed parts can already carry the information needed to reconstruct a word's bytes. Codex tried to remove a second spelling-to-word association while keeping the semantic word-to-object definition. In `18c16d0bf9`, native nomination ignored the caller's text label, resolved observed parts against the current catalog, and compared their row IDs with the basic percept of each live word. A `shopping` probe supplied an unrelated label and still recovered the observed spelling. That probe established label independence for a single cover.

**Why it was rolled back.** A tuple of row IDs changes when the same content acquires a new cover. In the CPU `grounded_model(pool=8)` fixture, recurrent reading of the byte rows for `love` produced word 9. After explicit catalog admission, recurrent reading of its aggregate row with the same whole properties produced word 10. The initial rows were `[0,1,2,3]` and the catalog constituents were `[4,5,6,7,8]`. The exact reproduction returned 9 then 9 on parent `3990759140`, 9 then 10 on the experimental revision, and **9 then 9 after rollback**.

**What a future attempt must preserve.** The recurrent-row regression checks the catalog part IDs, identical membership evidence from byte and aggregate reads, and the absence of further allocation. It now explicitly checks the returned identities too. The definition test reads a word again through its aggregate after it has a distinct witnessed object, and checks both directions of the association. A future proposal for label-independent nomination needs to satisfy these checks across a change of cover. [Recurrent-row regression](../../test/test_order0_percept_definitions.py) · [Definition tests](../../test/test_definition_rows.py)

**Rollback verification at `57a103f49f`.** 33 focused tests passed across percept definitions, lexical references, checkpoint restore, attention padding, omission and whole-sentence inverse reconstruction. The standalone CPU `love` reproduction also passed. The production-code edits relative to `3990759140` match the additions and deletions in the later sync commit, file by file.
