**Names.** A *behavioral chain* is the MTM entry: a sequence of order-0 states. A *chained event concept* is what a recurring behavioral chain promotes to. The bare word "chain" is not used for either, because it already names an uncompressed sentence's LTM1 reference tree (`ChainRead`) and expectation's sequence of end states (`test_ltm_chain_grows.py`).

## 1. The record (Alec, 2026-11-03)

1. "We might also have MTM operate on order zero concepts as a way of creating behavioral chains? Animals have that, and we might want to store those in MTM before promotion to chained event concepts."
2. "Behavioral chains should be implemented before step 0. Just as it identifies across nouns currently, it can also be used to identify 'the same' event (via gestalt properties, etc), which might even help in the creation of nouns."
3. The constraints this sits beside, from the same day: "MTM is not involved in perception/reconstruction, but only involved in thought and action"; "the identity store operates only on concepts of order-1 and higher"; the reconciliation recorded in §8: a chain at order 0 is a sequence, not an identity, so MTM's order-0 contents are chains, never order-0 identities.
4. The acceptance check of the order-zero loop that this must keep true: ablate STM and MTM, and reconstruction is unchanged.
5. The four things this design must specify (§8): what an order-0 chain is in MTM's terms, with its states, its `.when` stamps and its continuity rule; the gestalt properties by which two occurrences are the same event, and how that matching relates to the bind of nouns; the promotion rule and the LTM0 and LTM1 rows it writes; how an identity threaded through a chain becomes a noun candidate.

**Reading of "the same event" against Orders §1.** Orders says there is no "same" at order 0, only overlap and containment, and that continuity (the `.where`/`.when` trajectory) is order 1's evidence for collecting occurrences into an individual. A behavioral chain is that continuity evidence itself, held in MTM before any symbol collects it: the pre-symbolic stitching of located occurrences, as an identity's mention sequence is the stitching of mentions before promotion ([identity threading](2026-10-23-identity-threading.md) "the stitched chain"). Threading an occurrence into a behavioral chain is a judgment taken in thought at the kept close, with a withhold option, like the bind. It is not a second identity mechanism and it never runs in the percept pass.

## 3. The design, in code names

### 3.1 The entry

A behavioral chain is a `Token` keyed `('chain', n)` from a new `chain_counter`, beside `('candidate', n)`. It has `occurrences=()`, `kind=None`, `order=None`, `pending=True`, a transient id from `_allocate_id()`, `where` and `when` equal to its latest state's, and one new field `states: tuple = ()` of `(occurrence, when: int, where: Tensor[4])`, validated like `_value` is: `sentence(occurrence)`, strictly increasing `when`, a four-vector `where`. The discriminator is `key[0] == 'chain'`, so every reader that means "identity" by `entry.order is None` keeps its meaning, and no state is an `Event`: `token_of`'s fourth case, `accessible`'s OCCURRENCE scan, `_participants`, `context_records`, `promote_identity`'s fold and `TimelineRead` stay blind to chains.

New branches: `_detach` pops and releases the id as the candidate branch does; `close` gives a chain no default access (access only through `references`, as candidates); `_room` reads `entry.when` as the last state; `state()`/`restore()` move to version 11 (`('chain', n)` with `n < chain_counter`, `occurrences == ()`, states validated; versions 8 to 10 still accepted); `merge` and `forget` extend to states (`forget` drops a reclaimed state's address). Unchanged, and certified unchanged: `PrependedSymbols.stage`'s key filter, so chains are never prepended (D9); `committed_chunks` and `chunk_token`, so a chain is never a bind target and has no `IDENTITY_REF` code; `ReferenceClose.address` and `origin_reference`, so chains stay off the journal; `ConceptForgetting`, until promotion.

Chains share `mtmCapacity`, `mtmHorizon` and `transient_ids` with identities and candidates, as candidates and timelines do today. No new knob.

### 3.2 States and their `.when` (required content 1)

A state is the kept sentence's own LTM0 idea. At the end of `ReferenceClose.commit`, and at the end of the non-component branch, the occurrence is `store.occurrence_of(row)` for the `row` that `_commit_sentence` hands to `commit`. It is admitted as a state only when `store.order_of_row(row) == 0` (a lowered NP+VP; a modal or unlowered idea at order 1 or above is no state, [verb and modal §4.2](2026-10-23-verb-and-modal.md)) and `store.kind_of(row)` is not an estimate, a question or a cover. Its `when` is the sentence entry's stamp, decoded: `int(encoder().decode_index(store.entry_when(row)))`, following the layout rule that the sentence entry alone carries `.when`; not `commit`'s second tick. Its `where` is `clause.where` from `ClauseJournal.bands`, the same four coordinates `keep_close` stores. No new LTM0 row is written for a state; a state is a reference to an idea that exists.

### 3.3 Continuity (required content 1, and D3)

One open behavioral chain per stream. A new `mtm.extend_chain(stream, occurrence, *, when, where, value)` extends the stream's open chain when the new state is the next kept close of that stream (`closes[stream]` has advanced by one), lies within the same `DocumentCover.latest(store, stream)` cover, and its where band overlaps or abuts the last state's on the four ladder coordinates (exact, or a tolerance decided under D4). Otherwise the stream's open chain is left to expire and a new one opens with this state. Each extension is a `keep_access`, so an unextended chain expires after `horizon` kept closes like an unreferenced identity and competes for the same seats. A chain of one state is a chain; it is just never promoted (§3.5).

### 3.4 Event identity by gestalt (required content 2, and D4)

Recurrence is the second way a state reaches a chain: a new state that is not the continuation of the open chain may be the first state of a stored chain happening again. `MediumTermMemory.match_chain(value, where, when, *, stream)` returns a feature row per accessible chain, shaped like `match_candidate` and scored like the bind:

- **content:** `meet_ratios(coordinates(value), coordinates(state_value))` against the chain's next expected state (its first state for a fresh recurrence, the state after the last matched one while a recurrence is in progress);
- **proximity:** overlap of the where bands;
- **adjacency:** the `recall_time` window on decoded ticks;
- **common fate:** equality of the first difference, the new state against its predecessor compared with the chain's corresponding link; from `Event.before`/`after` where a subject exists, else the difference of consecutive state ideas.

Relation to the bind of nouns: the same decision shape. One feature row per accessible chain through a small `policy`, a zero-logit dummy meaning "no recurrence; open or extend as §3.3 says", chosen at the kept close in thought, never in the percept pass, and credited by the ordinary departure rule like every other choice (item 2.0 §2 and §3: one proposal family, one comparison, one credit). The band terms live outside `IdentityRows.coordinates`, which stays as it is; the bind of nouns gains no location term in this round (D4b). No revision: a state joined to the wrong chain stays there, as a mention joined to the wrong identity does ([Future work](../FutureWork.md) "Re-identification in MTM").

A recurrence in progress is one `record_reading(key, stream, pair)` per matched state, so `count = len(readings)` within the horizon counts recurrences of the chain.

### 3.5 Recurrence and promotion (required content 3, and D5)

`promote_candidates` gains a third branch, `key[0] == 'chain'`: promote when `count >= owner.concept_mint_count` and `len(states) >= 2`; the gate is unconditional, like identities, not `attentionPromotion` (D5a). Promotion writes:

- **LTM0:** nothing. The states are ideas already.
- **LTM1:** one link row per consecutive pair, `alloc.parts.add((OCCURRENCE, s_i[-1]), (OCCURRENCE, s_{i+1}[-1]), truth_plus=1., when=stamp(s_{i+1}), where=where(s_{i+1}), witness=True)`; a repeated link is witnessed and its `access_count` bumps. Plus one membership row per state, `(OCCURRENCE, s_i) -> (SYMBOL, cid)`, so `class_members` and `cover_members` read the concept's members as they do an identity's (D5b: the flat pair plus membership rows, rather than the nested `(OCC s_i, REL (SYM cid, OCC s_{i+1}))` form).
- **The concept:** `owner._preflight_concept_row` and `_preflight_concept_allocation`, `cid = alloc.new_concept()`, `add_constituent(cid, 'part', ('occ', s[-1]))` per state, so `_part_order` derives order 1 from the `('occ', ...)` parts and `reference_orders[cid]` records it (D5c: order 1, the class of its recurrences, the one-member paradigm of Philosophy's "identity across time" become many-member; an individual is the same construction over one worldline); `settle`; `_csw_concept_row(order, cid)`; `symbol_types[cid] = {'concept'}` (not an object; it joins no `individuals`); form `_fold(owner, store, state rows)`; `remember_content`; `place_rows([row], *_mtm_placement_contexts[batch])`; `pool.promote(key, cid)` releases the transient id; `relate_idx['part-base', cid] = ('chain', n)`; `interpret.admit_discovered()`.

Readers need no change: `ChainRead.components/head_idea` read the link rows, `recall_time` returns the states, `recall_identities` and `TimelineRead.read`'s LTM1 fallback are untouched. `PresentationMemory.epoch` withdraws the `('occ', ...)` grounds and keeps `reference_orders`, as for every promoted identity. `ConceptForgetting` protects `entry.symbol` as today.

### 3.6 Noun candidacy (required content 4, and D6)

An identity token whose occurrences are the subject or object events of at least `concept_mint_count` consecutive states of one chain (read from `mtm.entries[token].events`, with `event.occurrence in chain.states`) receives one `refinement['mentions'][occurrence] = (stream, clock)` witness per such state. That is the count `promote_candidates` already reads for identities, so `promote_identity` mints it with no new count and no new rows. The alternative is the raise, `raiseCount` rows `(OCC event_i, SYM identity)` (Orders §11). Recommended: minting through the existing witness count. Whether this helps create nouns is a learning measurement for item 0, recorded null if null.

### 3.7 Existing order-0 contents (D7) and forgetting (D8)

"MTM's order-0 contents are chains only" is read as: no order-0 identity is opened. That is enforced where it is today (`ChunkJournal.apply` 178, `push` 70) and certified: no `('ltm', ...)` token is opened for a row with `order_of_row == 0` except one of `kind == NULL_CONCEPT`. Literal candidates (`order=0`, under `conceptualPi`) and sign and no-op tokens stay as they are; they are not reclassified as one-state chains. Chain state occurrences join `Forgetting.model_boundary`'s roots as `_inter_context_occurrences` do (D8, recommended), so a forgetting pass does not orphan a state of a live chain; the alternative lets `compact_rows -> mtm.forget` drop a reclaimed state.

### 3.8 Credit and training

Chain decisions are choices and take the ordinary departure credit; under item 2.0 §2 that credit is on the output side whole. Every MTM write stays detached at the close. Chain states supply before and after pairs, which is the material of the state-of-affairs training of verbs (item 2.0 §5, §8); that training is the order-zero loop's part of item 2.0 and is not built by this design. No teacher term is introduced.

### 3.9 Not in this round

Prepending chains to the next sentence's field; chain expectation (`_inter_context` remains expectation's context); state-of-affairs training of verbs from chain pairs; re-identification; any change to `conceptualPi`; branching chains (a chain holds the actual sequence; alternative-timeline ideas are above order 0 and are never states).
