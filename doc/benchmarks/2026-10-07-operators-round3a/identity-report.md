Sparse forms address the index. The fixed dense projection supplies binding directions. The full corpus census is a static construction audit and adds no training.

The tables below retain the frozen report's raw double-precision fit. The
[stored-precision analysis](precision-geometry.md) qualifies rank on float32
data, particularly the sum control's rounding residual. The
[centered operator spectra](operator-geometry.md) compare conjunction and
disjunction using saved roots. Neither post-hoc report changes a gate result.

The configured BasicModel corpus contains more words than its declared 32,768-row part bank can admit. The census uses a separate audit bank to measure forms and reports its row count. This round does not resize the production bank or claim a full-corpus training pass.

The same toy sample has 19,986 unique words, 1 collision group before minting and 0 after. calaba/cabala use cal@1/cab@1, three bits each. Every required witness bank records this mint too.

Both projected conjunction and disjunction roots have affine rank four on the four gate sentences. Their centered nonzero condition numbers are 1.82 and 11.75 respectively. Disjunction is less well conditioned but remains separable well above float32 noise. A conjunction-only flip criterion is therefore a historical diagnostic; the finite-step preference observed here does not itself certify semantic operator behavior.
