closing preview's selected records. The full own-description mass is the
denominator; coordinates absent from a narrower candidate are uncovered.

working value. Own objects, mints, dummy choices and gaps pay nothing.
Arrival items and thought episodes are outside the sum. No name cost,
stored working vector, MTM read, detach or chooser change is added.

registers `reconstruction.reference` once, with baseline one and the active
